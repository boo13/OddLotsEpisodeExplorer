## Show Format & Style

### Episode Types
1. **Deep Dive Interviews** - Single guest exploring one topic in depth
2. **"Lots More" Episodes** - Shorter follow-ups on breaking news or listener questions
3. **Special Episodes** - Live events, year-end reviews, prediction episodes

### Content Approach
- **One topic per episode** examined in detail
- Topics range from macro (Fed policy, China's economy) to niche (chicken prices, nickel mining, shipping containers)
- Frequent use of expert guests: economists, traders, academics, policymakers, industry specialists
- Known for finding interesting angles on stories others miss

### Tone
- Conversational but substantive
- Curious and intellectually playful
- Accessible to general audiences while still valuable to finance professionals
